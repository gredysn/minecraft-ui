## Student Notes
If you have any bonus specs, bonus or any details the TA's should know, you should include it here:

For our bonus we included the PowerPuff Girls.

Implementations:
- The PowerPuff Girls were added as items.
- The ingredients for the PowerPuff Girls were added: Sugar, Spice, Everything Nice, Chemical X, and three special items corresponding to each PowerPuff Girl (Blossom Flower for Blossom, Bubbles for Bubbles, and Butter for Buttercup).
- Recipes for each PowerPuff Girl were added:
    -Blossom:
        (Sugar)           (Spice)            (Sugar)
    (Blossom Flower)  (Everything Nice)  (Blossom Flower)
      (Chemical X)       (Chemical X)      (Chemical X)

    -Bubbles:
      (Sugar)            (Spice)         (Sugar)
     (Bubbles)      (Everything Nice)   (Bubbles)
    (Chemical X)      (Chemical X)     (Chemical X)

    -Buttercup:
      (Sugar)          (Spice)           (Sugar)
      (Butter)     (Everything Nice)     (Butter)
    (Chemical X)      (Chemical X)     (Chemical X)

- A shortcut was added in order to get the ingredients necessary to make all three PowerPuff Girls. Just press 'P' or 'p'.
- A new "Battle" state was added, along with its button.
- In the Battle state you are able to place the PowerPuff Girls in the slots provided in any order.
- The PowerPuff Girls can battle and defeat a villain. Just press 'A' or 'a' to attack the villain.
